# HyperLearn — 30 s film, storyboard draft

One continuous visual idea: **orbit → path → section → agent node → citation → shared branch**.
The HyperLearn mark itself is a path with a node between two bars; the film builds toward it.

| Time | Narration | Visual | Motion | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–4.0 | "The world never stops. It keeps changing." | A clean vector globe (blue continents, white ocean, hairline graticule) turns in real 3D (orthographic projection). Three tilted orbit rings; small circular symbol chips ride them (book, compass, flask, laptop, phone, wrench, chart). When a chip passes behind the globe it comes back as its newer counterpart (book → tablet, compass → map pin, wrench → chip). Four people stand in a loose arc in front, each with subtle idle motion. | Globe spins continuously; rings precess slightly; parallax between globe, rings and people. No zoom-only shots. | Low pulse every chip swap; soft pad enters. |
| 4.0–8.0 | "Those who don't learn to adapt risk being left behind. Don't let that happen to you." | Orbits flatten and unroll into several routes on a white plane, running left to right. The others keep moving, on their own routes, not racing. | Camera trucks right with the routes; on "Don't let that happen to you" it settles on the student at the gap; the gap ends pulse once. | Pad thins out; a single held note at the gap. |
