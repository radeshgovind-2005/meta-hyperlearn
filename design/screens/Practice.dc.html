Question 2 · in the style of Midterm 2025

Let A = [[4, 1], [2, 3]]. Find the eigenvalues of A and one eigenvector for each.

Your answer

det(A − λI) = (4 − λ)(3 − λ) + 2
λ² − 7λ + 14 = 0

Not quite

The off-diagonal product is subtracted, not added: (4 − λ)(3 − λ) − 2. That gives λ² − 7λ + 10 = 0, so λ = 2 and λ = 5. See L05_slides, page 4
