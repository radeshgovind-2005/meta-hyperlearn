Eigenvectors and eigenvalues

In 3.1 you saw that a matrix moves vectors around: it can stretch them, flip them and turn them.

A nonzero vector v is an eigenvector of A if Av = λv for some scalar λ. The eigenvalues are the roots of the characteristic polynomial det(A − λI), and together they form the spectrum of A.1,2
