Why do eigenvectors matter for PCA?

Read 6 sources: 2 from your library, 4 from the web.

PCA looks for the directions along which your data varies the most. Those directions are the eigenvectors of the data’s covariance matrix.13

Each eigenvalue tells you how much variance lies along its eigenvector, so sorting by eigenvalue ranks the directions from most to least informative.24

Because a covariance matrix is symmetric, these eigenvectors are orthogonal. That is the spectral theorem from section 3.4 of your course.2
