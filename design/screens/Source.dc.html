2.1 Eigenvectors

Most vectors change direction when multiplied by A.

Definition: a nonzero x with Ax = λx is an eigenvector of A, and λ is its eigenvalue.

Example: for A = [[2, 0], [0, 3]], e₁ and e₂ are eigenvectors.
